# Digital Mahendra

> **Make growth feel obvious.**  
> AI-powered marketing systems for small businesses—clear strategy, connected tools, and practical next steps.

Digital Mahendra helps small businesses bring marketing channels, offers, and AI tools together around one focused growth goal. The website presents a clean, dark visual identity with cyan accents, subtle motion, and a straightforward path to get in touch.

## What you’ll find here

A responsive, single-page portfolio website built with plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. It includes:

- A mobile-friendly navigation and animated growth dashboard
- Marketing services and a four-step process overview
- A free AI tools guide signup form
- Contact and custom growth system inquiry sections
- FAQs, client-story cards, and clear calls to action
- Scroll reveals, animated counters, and responsive layouts

## Services

| Focus | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| Meta Ads | Campaign setup and optimization |
| Growth strategy | Funnels, content, SEO, and offers |
| Email marketing | Subscriber and customer journeys |
| AI tools setup | Practical tools and workflow automation |
| Product campaigns | Launch planning and promotion |
| Analytics | Reporting to guide the next move |

## Getting started

Open `index.html` in a browser to preview the site. For contact and guide form submissions, serve the page over HTTP or HTTPS. The forms use FormSubmit and require the recipient’s initial activation email to be approved before submissions are delivered.
